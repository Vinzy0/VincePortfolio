## Design Context

### Users
All audiences in parallel: recruiters/hiring managers doing a quick vibe-check and looking for contact info; freelance clients and collaborators evaluating creative fit; designer/developer peers who will notice and appreciate the craft of the site itself. Because peers are in the mix, the site itself IS portfolio work — the medium is the message.

### Brand Personality
Playful · Expressive · Artsy-nerdy. The hand-drawn rough.js aesthetic is the central design decision and should be treated as the brand's signature, not decoration. The site should feel like Vince made it by hand — like a sketchbook that happens to run in a browser. Warm, a little weird, distinctly personal.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark background, space/constellation motif, rough.js SVG hand-drawn elements throughout. The chatbot-as-portfolio-centrepiece is a strong, unconventional concept — the entire site is one conversation. Anti-reference: no generic dev portfolio (hero image + card grid + contact section), no clinical minimalism, no overused purple/blue glowing neon aesthetic.

### Design Principles
1. **The medium is the message** — every pixel should feel hand-crafted, not generated. If it could come from a template, rethink it.
2. **Warmth over polish** — a little roughness is intentional. Perfection would kill the vibe. Imperfect lines, slight asymmetry, and personality beats sterile cleanliness.
3. **Curiosity-first UX** — the chatbot interaction model rewards exploration. Nothing should feel like a form to fill out.
4. **Distinctive > safe** — the site should make someone ask "how was this made?" not "which template is this."
5. **Accessible weirdness** — the hand-drawn aesthetic doesn't excuse keyboard traps or invisible focus states. Personality and accessibility aren't at odds.
